# Security policy

This policy applies to every public StackLLM repository that does not define its own.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please **do not open a public issue** for a security problem.

Report it privately:

- by email to [oussamakanouni39@gmail.com](mailto:oussamakanouni39@gmail.com), with "Security" in the subject;
- or, on a repository where it is enabled, through GitHub's **Report a vulnerability** button (Security tab).

Please include what you found, where, and how to reproduce it. Do not access or change data that is not yours.

## What to expect

StackLLM is run by one person, so reports are handled on a best-effort basis. You will get an answer, and the issue will be fixed and disclosed responsibly once it is understood. There is no bug bounty.

## Scope

Public StackLLM repositories only. Products that are not yet in production are not exposed to the public, and their private repositories are out of scope.
